# 🐝 THE HIVE

> **One Queen. One Wasp. Everyone else is a Bee.**
>
> You don't know who to trust.

THE HIVE is a real-time social deduction game set inside a massive honeycomb-like hive.

There are no meetings.  
There are no voting screens.  
There is no convenient button saying **"WHO IS THE SUSSY ONE?"**

Players move through the hive, observe each other, lie, stalk, cooperate, betray, and fight for survival.

---

## 🐝 The Roles

### 👑 Queen

The Queen is the most important player in the Hive.

She knows she is the Queen.

She does **not** know who the Wasp is.

Her primary objective is simple:

**Survive.**

But staying hidden becomes increasingly difficult as the game progresses.

---

### 🐝 Bees

Everyone else begins as a Bee.

Each Bee:

- Has **1 sting per phase**
- Knows they are a Bee
- Does not know who the Queen is
- Does not know who the Wasp is

Bees must cooperate carefully.

Because killing the wrong person can be catastrophic.

---

### 🐝 Wasp

There is exactly **one Wasp**.

The Wasp knows they are the Wasp.

They do **not** know who the Queen is.

The Wasp has:

- **3 stings per phase**
- The ability to kill players
- The ability to sabotage active chambers

The Wasp wins by eliminating the Queen or successfully sabotaging an active chamber.

---

# ⚔️ The Phases

## I. INTRUSION

The Wasp has infiltrated the Hive.

The Queen is hiding.

The Bees are hunting.

The Wasp has **3 stings**.

Every Bee has **1 sting**.

### Wasp objectives

The Wasp can win by:

- Killing the Queen
- Successfully sabotaging an active chamber

If either happens:

> **Everyone dies. The Wasp wins.**

### Bee objective

Kill the Wasp.

But beware.

If a Bee kills the Queen before the Wasp is eliminated:

> **The Wasp wins.**

This means the Queen's identity is valuable information.

And extremely dangerous information.

---

## II. ASSASSINATION

If the Bees successfully kill the Wasp, the game enters:

**ASSASSINATION**

The Wasp is gone.

The Queen is still alive.

Now the Bees no longer need to protect her.

They need to find her.

And kill her.

### The rule

**Whoever kills the Queen wins.**

When the Queen dies:

> **Everyone except the killer dies.**

There is no shared victory.

No second place.

No congratulations from the Hive.

One winner.

---

## III. TRUE COLOURS

Sometimes the Hive becomes much quieter.

If every Bee is eliminated while both the Queen and Wasp remain alive, the game enters:

**TRUE COLOURS**

The deception is over.

The Queen is exposed.

The Wasp now has the entire Hive to hunt her.

The Queen must run.

The Wasp must find her.

This phase is governed by a time limit.

---

# 🍯 The Hive

The game takes place inside a large interconnected honeycomb structure.

The map contains multiple chambers, including areas such as:

- 🌱 Nursery
- 🌻 Garden
- 📦 Storage
- 🐝 Central Hive
- 🔧 Workshop
- 💧 Water
- 👑 Queen Chamber

Some chambers can become **ACTIVE**.

Active chambers are important because the Wasp can sabotage them during **INTRUSION**.

The Hive itself is designed around:

- Hexagonal rooms
- Honeycomb architecture
- Amber lighting
- Dark wooden structures
- Glowing hive infrastructure
- Interconnected pathways

---

# 🎮 Gameplay

THE HIVE focuses on **real-time social deduction**.

Players can:

- Explore the Hive
- Follow other players
- Hide
- Observe suspicious behaviour
- Lie about identities
- Protect the Queen
- Hunt the Wasp
- Hunt the Queen
- Use their limited stings
- Move between chambers
- React to changing phases

There are no traditional meetings or voting rounds.

The Hive never stops moving.

---

# 🧠 The Information Game

The most important resource in THE HIVE isn't ammunition.

It's information.

During INTRUSION, a player might claim:

> "They're the Queen."

That statement could be true.

It could be a lie.

It could be an attempt to protect the real Queen.

Or it could be a trap.

The Queen's identity becomes increasingly valuable as the game progresses.

The same information can therefore help one player while destroying another.
